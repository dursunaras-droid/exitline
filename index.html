Support

ExitLine is a single-player puzzle game. Every level is a silhouette drawn out of arrow lines; you tap the lines in the order that lets each one leave the board.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, refund or purchase problems — write to us and we answer within a few business days.

[E-POSTA]

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version and the level number.

I bought “Remove Ads” but ads are still showing

Open Settings in the game (gear icon, top right) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase. Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase — it never expires and it is not a subscription.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on the device itself. Deleting the app, or restoring the device without a backup, removes it. Signing in is not required to play, so we cannot recover progress from our side.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All levels are included in the app and play fully offline. A connection is only used to load ads and to complete a purchase.

I am stuck on a level

Use the hint button in the game bar: it finds a line that can leave right now, brings the camera to it and dims everything else. Hints are also available by watching a rewarded ad.

Are the puzzles always solvable?

Yes. Every level ships with a verified solution; no level can reach a dead end that forces a restart.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

ExitLine is published by [ŞİRKET ADI] (“we”). This policy explains what happens to data when you use the app.

We do not ask for any personal information

There is no account, no sign-up, no login and no profile. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location, and we do not collect them.

What stays on your device

Your completed levels, best results, sound / haptics / theme preferences and daily hint counts are stored locally on the device. They never leave it and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process technical data such as a device advertising identifier, IP address, device type, operating system version and interactions with the ad. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

  • Google’s handling of this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can limit ad personalisation on iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and Apple Advertising.
  • Buying Remove Ads stops the ads shown between levels.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, card number or billing address — the app only learns whether the purchase is active on your Apple ID.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app includes Google Firebase libraries, but analytics and crash collection are switched off and no events or crash reports are sent. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

ExitLine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to export or delete. For requests about advertising data, contact Google using the link above, or write to us at [E-POSTA].

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new date above.
