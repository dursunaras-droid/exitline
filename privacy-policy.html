ExitLine (“the app”) is published by [ŞİRKET ADI] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app touches, who processes it, and what your options are. It applies to the ExitLine app on the Apple App Store.

The short version

No account. No sign-up. We do not ask for or collect your name, e-mail, phone number or location. Your game progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes technical device data to serve them. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information about you. The app has no account system, no login, no contact form and no profile. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not build a profile of you.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on the device itself:

  • Which levels you have completed, and your best result on them
  • Your preferences: sound, haptics, high contrast, light/dark theme
  • Daily hint and eraser counts
  • Whether the “Remove Ads” purchase is active on this device

This data never leaves the device, is not sent to us, and is not readable by us. Deleting the app deletes it. Because it is not backed up to an account, we cannot restore it for you.

3. Advertising

The free version of the app displays ads supplied by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC), which acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. To deliver, cap and measure ads, Google may process:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA / device identifier)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement
IP address, coarse regionServing locally appropriate ads, fraud prevention
Device model, OS version, app versionAd compatibility and rendering
Ad interactions (views, taps, rewarded-ad completions)Measurement and billing

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. How Google uses it is described here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • On iOS you can turn off app tracking under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and limit Apple’s own ad personalisation under Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes the ads shown between levels.
  • Rewarded ads (for extra hints) are always optional — nothing in the game is locked behind watching one.

4. Purchases

“Remove Ads” is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. The entire payment is processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your card details, billing address or Apple ID. The app only learns from Apple whether the purchase is currently active, so it can stop showing ads. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

The app bundles Google Firebase libraries (Analytics and Crashlytics), but data collection for both is disabled: no analytics events and no crash reports are transmitted. If a future version enables them, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted with a new date at the top of this page.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not hold any. The only third parties involved are Google (advertising, as described above) and Apple (app distribution and payments), each under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

7. Data retention

We retain nothing, because nothing reaches our servers — the app has no server of its own. Data held on your device is kept until you delete the app. Retention of advertising data is determined by Google.

8. Children

ExitLine is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party ad, contact us and we will help you raise it with the provider.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, KVKK and similar laws you have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to export, correct or erase. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the Google links above, or write to us and we will point you to the right process.

10. International use

The app can be used anywhere the App Store operates. Any processing by Google or Apple may take place in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

Any update is published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes will also be described in the App Store release notes of the version they apply to.
