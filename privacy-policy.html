Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

ExitLine (“the app”) is published by Dursun Aras (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app touches, who processes it, and what your options are. It applies to the ExitLine app on the Apple App Store.

The short version

  • You can play the whole game without an account and without giving us any personal information.
  • Your progress is stored on your device. It only leaves the device if you choose Save Progress with Apple, which backs it up so a reinstall can restore it.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes technical device data to serve them.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.
  • You can erase everything yourself, in the app, with Settings › Delete My Data.

1. Information we collect

Playing requires no account, no sign-up and no personal information. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location in order to play, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not build an advertising profile of you.

The only case in which we hold data about you is if you deliberately turn on the optional progress backup described in section 3.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on the device itself:

  • Which levels you have completed, and your best result on them
  • Your preferences: sound, haptics, high contrast, light/dark theme
  • Daily hint and eraser counts
  • Whether the “Remove Ads” purchase is active on this device

Deleting the app deletes this local copy.

3. Optional: Save Progress with Apple

Because deleting the app also deletes your progress, Settings offers an optional backup called Save Progress with Apple. It is off until you tap it. If you use it:

  • The app asks Apple to confirm your identity through Sign in with Apple. Apple lets you choose whether to share your name with us or hide it; we do not require it, and we do not ask for your e-mail address.
  • Your progress — completed levels, best moves and best times — is then also stored in our cloud database, operated with Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC acting as our processor), tied to an identifier derived from that Apple ID.
  • This exists for one purpose only: giving your own progress back to you after a reinstall or on a new device. We do not sell it, share it, or use it to contact or profile you.
  • It is not a social account: there is no public profile, no friends list and no leaderboard tied to it.

If you never use this option, no progress data ever leaves your device.

4. Deleting your data

Open Settings in the game and tap Delete My Data. This immediately and permanently erases your cloud-stored progress, the Apple ID link and the local save on that device. No request to us is required and there is nothing left for us to hold afterwards.

You can also revoke the app's access from your Apple ID at any time under iOS Settings › [your name] › Sign in with Apple.

5. Advertising

The free version of the app displays ads supplied by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC), which acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. To deliver, cap and measure ads, Google may process:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA / device identifier)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement
IP address, coarse regionServing locally appropriate ads, fraud prevention
Device model, OS version, app versionAd compatibility and rendering
Ad interactions (views, taps, rewarded-ad completions)Measurement and billing

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. The app requests non-personalised ads and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

On iOS you can turn off app tracking under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and limit Apple's own ad personalisation under Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes the ads shown between levels. Rewarded ads (for extra hints) are always optional — nothing in the game is locked behind watching one.

6. Purchases

“Remove Ads” is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. The entire payment is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details, card number or billing address. The app only learns whether the purchase is active on your Apple ID.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

The app includes Google Firebase libraries. Analytics and crash collection are switched off, and no analytics events or crash reports are sent. Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are used only for the optional progress backup described in section 3, and Firebase Remote Config is used to adjust ad pacing settings. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

8. Children

ExitLine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

If you have never used Save Progress with Apple, we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing on our side to export or delete. If you have used it, you can delete all of it yourself at any time via Settings › Delete My Data, or write to us and we will do it for you. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights of access, correction, portability and objection under laws such as the GDPR or KVKK — write to us and we will respond.

For requests about advertising data held by Google, use the Google link in section 5.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
